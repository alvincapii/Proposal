You Miss my Hugs

Hello loveloveee, if I could, I'd stop whatever I'm doing right now just to come over and give you the biggest, longest hug ever. Yung type ng yakap na pareho tayong ayaw na bumitaw :< I know there are days when words aren't enough, and sometimes all you really want is to have someone beside you, holding you close. I wish I could be that person physically, especially on the days when things feel a little heavier than usual. I hope you know that when we finally get to be together, I'm going to make up for all the hugs we've missed. All those little moments when you wanted to lean on me, hold my hand, or simply rest your head on my shoulder. I want to experience all of those with you :< For now, imagine mo po Patpat wrapping his arms around you, gently patting your head, and telling you that everything's going to be okay. You don't have to say anything or explain how you're feeling. You can just stay there for as long as you want and Patpat will hug you tight. Patpat will kiss your forehead before letting you go tooo. I love you always my love, This is the quote I came across with for this letter: "Absence is to love what wind is to fire; it extinguishes the small, it enkindles the great." I chose this because I think it describes something about us that I don’t always know how to put into words and hindi ko masyado nasasabi sayo. Being apart isn’t always easy, may times na super miss na natin isa’t isa, and sometimes yakap lang ng isa’t isa yung need natin. I really like this quote because of what it says about being apart from someone you love. the way i understand it, the wind has two effects on a fire. it can put out a small flame, but when the fire is strong enough, it only makes it burn even brighter. It says that love can be the same way, distance can make feelings that aren’t deep go and fade away, pero yung love like us na genuine, being apart will make it grow even stronger. I hope that when you miss my hugs, you remember that there's someone on the other side who misses holding you just as much. Someone who's looking forward to all the ordinary moments with you, even the ones that might seem insignificant to anyone else. I miss holding you, lovelove ko. Hopefully, it won't be too long before I can hold and hug you again. Until then, Patpat will be there for you muna :< I love you, baby.
With love, Alvin
