You Need Someone to Sit in Silence With

Hi lovelove, if you’re opening this, maybe you don’t really feel like talking right now. Maybe you’re tired, maybe your mind feels wayyyy too loud for you to form any proper thoughts. Maybe there’s a lot of things you don’t know how to explain, or maybe you don’t want to explain them to me yet, and that’s okay. You don’t have to explain sa ngayon love, I want you to know that if I was there with you right now, I wouldn’t force you to talk and just give you hugs. I wouldn’t ask you a hundred questions like what I used to do, I wouldn’t make you feel like you have to hurry and feel better. Me would just sit beside you, in silence. I understand that there are moments where you feel like you have to carry everything by yourself, because no one else will understand. Moments where explaining what you feel also feels exhausting. But I hope this letter reminds you that you don’t always have to fight for my attention, okay? You just have to tell me when you need me, love. You don't have to be smiling for me to want to spend time with you. You don't have to entertain me, make me laugh, or carry every conversation. I love you when you’re sad, when you’re overwhelmed, when you’re frustrated with me, when you don’t feel okay, and when you feel depressed. I will never get tired of reminding you that sa’yo love. None of those versions of you are any less deserving of love, and none of them make me want to leave. If today feels overwhelming, please don't convince yourself that you have to carry it alone. You don't have to have everything figured out tonight. You don't have to rush yourself into feeling okay just because you think you should. So if you’re reading this ngayon lovelove ko, imagine me sitting beside you okay? Imagine me gently holding your hand, and giving you headpats. We’d simply sit in silence and find comfort in the presence of each other. Take all the time that you need, love. Whenever you’re ready, I’ll be here ready to listen. I want to remind you that even when we’re separated by distance, I will never let you sit in silence completely alone again like before. I love you always lovelove ko.
With love, Alvin
