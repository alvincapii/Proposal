We Are Busy

Hi love, if you’re opening this, then life is probably getting in the way of us ngayon. Maybe we are both doing schoolwork, maybe you’re studying and I’m doing work, maybe one of us is outside ngayon, or just one of those days where everything feels overwhelming. Maybe yung replies din natin ngayon are slower than usual, or we barely had the time to hear each other’s voice. Being busy doesn’t mean I love you any less, okay? No matter how hectic my day gets, there is ALWAYS a part of me that is thinking about you. I know that the distance can make it easy to overthink; what more pa kung busy tayo parehas diba? We might even feel lonely, or wonder when we finally have time together again. Please do not feel guilty for focusing on your studies or responsibilities baby; I want to see you succeed in life :> I want you to study well and take care of yourself, love. When things are better, I will still be here, excited to hear every little story you want to tell me. I’m so excited, so please tell me about your day, your random thoughts, something funny that happened, or even what you had for lunch at school. I love the little parts of your life too, lovelove. Until then, don’t forget to take breaks, okay? Drink some water, eat properly. Get enough rest after your busy work, love. If you ever start missing me, imagine me giving you the biggest hug and reminding you that we are going to be spending time again soon :> I love you during the long days, I love you during the short replies, I love you even when we can’t call for hours. You are always worth waiting for, lovelove ko. I love you always.
With love, Alvin
