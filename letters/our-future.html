You Think About Our Future

Helloo lovelove koo, if nababasa mo ‘to ngayon maybe you’re thinking about our future together, about what will happen to us, or kung paano ba talaga natin maabot yung future na lagi natin pinaguusapan together. Maybe you’re excited, maybe you’re worried, maybe you’re just wondering kung pano ba talaga natin yun magagawaaa. I’ll be honest kasi ako rin napapaisip minsan, I know our relationship isn’t always going to be easy, and there are definitely doubts kung kakayanin ba talaga natin, kung tayo pa rin talaga. We’re in different countries, we have our own responsibilities, our studies, our problems, and days when we won’t have as much time for each other as we’d like. There might be times na mamimiss natin ng sobra ang isa’t isa, there might be misunderstandings, difficult conversations, and moments na kailangan din natin maging patient sa isa’t isa. I can’t promise you baby na everything will always work out perfectly, pero what I can promise you is that alam ko na we’re both growing, we’re both learning, and we will both continue to figure out a lot of things about each other. I want to keep learning how to love you better, especially on the days when loving each other takes more effort than usual. Lagi ko naiisip yung future natin together love, I always think about the day na sa isa’t isa na tayo uuwi, yung mga tampuhan natin madali nalang ayusin kasi magkasama na tayo, pwede na kita bilhan palagi ng flowers and mga pasalubong, pwede kita i-date sa kahit saan natin gustuhin, pwede kitang pagsilbihan sa house natin and paglutuan kita ng masasarap na foods. Me looking forward so much na makasama na kita sa iisang bubong. Alam ko na we still have a lot of things to go through, we have dreams to chase, things to learn, and responsibilities na need pa natin gampanan, I want us to grow into the people we want to become, and I hope we can be part of each other’s growth without making either of us feel like we have to give up our own dreams just to prove our love to each other. i don't want you to feel like you have to be certain about everything just to stay with me. i want us to be honest about our fears, too. if you're worried, tell me. if something feels difficult, let's talk about it. if our plans change, let's figure out what comes next. i want a future where we can be honest with each other, even when the conversations aren't easy. Just like what I always say sa letters ko, in the end, I hope it’s still you and me, figuring it out together. I wanted to share this quote with you lovelove: “Love does not consist of gazing at each other, but in looking outward together in the same direction” kasi it reminded me that our relationship isn’t just about loving each other, but also growing together and working toward the future we both dream of. I know we still have sooooo many things to figure out, but as long as we keep supporting each other, learn from our mistakes, and become better versions of ourselves, everything will work out. I want to build a life with you, love, not just because I love you, but because you’re someone na gusto kong kasama sa tabi ko sa lahat ng pwedeng ibato sa akin ng buhay. Mahal na mahal kita love :<
With love, Alvin
