You Need Assurance

Hello baby kooo, if binabasa mo to me think na your mind is a little louder than usual, maybe you’re overthinking the little things tooo. Maybe you feel insecure, and you’re wondering if you’re enough. Maybe you’re scared too that things have changed, or that one day I’ll stop choosing you and leave you. Wag ikaw mag-alala lovelove, before your thoughts convince you of something na hindi totoo, I want you to at least hear my thoughts too. I love you. Not the version of you that’s always laughing and smiling. Hindi lang yung version mo na confident ikaw and feel mo pretty ikaw, hindi lang yung version mo na okay. I love all of you. I love you on the days when you’re happy just as much as I love you during the days you feel sad, jealous, overwhelmed, tired, and exhausted. Your difficult days do not make you any less loveable to me lovelove. They don’t make me want to leave, and they most certainly do not make me regret choosing you and staying. I know na your mind can sometimes tell you things that make you question everything, it tells you that someone else could make me happier, that maybe you’re too much, or that one day I’ll get tired of you, but those thoughts are not me, okay? If I wanted someone else, I wouldn’t still be here loving you, every single day. I wouldn’t be here making memories with you, writing you letters, working hard for our future, and choosing you every single day. I don’t stay because I have to, I stay because I want to. Lovelove ko, you’re the one person I imagine beside me in all the little moments life has yet to bring. So if today is one of those days where you’re doubting yourself, believe that I believe in you until you can believe in yourself again. I love you, and no amount of overthinking can change that, love. I love you always.
With love, Alvin
