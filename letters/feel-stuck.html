You Feel Stuck in Life

Hello lovelove, I know there are times when you look at other people and wonder why things seem to be so much easier for them. Why they get to experience things that you’ve been wanting to experience for so long too, and why everyone else seems to be moving forward while you feel like you’re stuck in the same place. I wish I could make you see yourself the way I see you, lovelove. I don’t see someone who’s falling behind; I see someone who’s trying, someone who’s been through things that other people might not even understand, and someone who still finds the strength to keep going kahit na mahirap madalas. I know you want to go to school, experience the little things, make memories, have the kind of normal days that you’ve always wished for. I know telling you to just be patient won’t make things easier, and I won’t pretend na naiintindihan ko completely lahat ng pinagdadaanan mo. But I hope you know na your circumstances don’t make you any less deserving of those experiences. Ang daming pinagkait sayo dahil kinuha ikaw sa Japan. Pero, you still have so many things ahead of you, so many places to see, memories to make, things to learn, and versions of yourself that you haven’t even met yet. Please don’t let the things you haven’t experienced make you forget all of the things you can still look forward to. You don’t have to catch up with anyone, you don’t have to rush yourself just because you’re afraid that time is passing by, okay? Let this letter be a gentle reminder to you that ang buhay ay hindi karera. I came across this quote: “A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step”. I think it’s worth remembering too love: you don’t have to see the entire path ahead of you ngayon; you don’t have to exactly know where you’ll be next; you’re not running out of time. All you need to do is take things one step at a time, and eventually, you’ll get where you want to be. Even on days when all you can manage is a tiny step, that still counts. Please don’t underestimate how far you’ve come just because you haven’t reached where you want to be yet. And love, if there's one thing I never want you to doubt, it's how much I love you. I love you not because of what you accomplish, how far you've come, or how much you manage to do in a day. I love you simply because you're you. even on days when you don't feel like your best self, even when you're struggling, even when you feel like you have nothing to be proud of. none of those things make me love you any less. I wish I could take away all the doubts you have about yourself, but since I can't, I hope I can remind you every day that you're someone worth loving. I hope you never feel like you have to achieve something first before you can be proud of yourself or feel deserving of love. I'm happy that it's you. and through all the things we have yet to experience, I still want it to be you. I love you love.
With love, Alvin
