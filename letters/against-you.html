You Feel The World Is Against You

Hiiii loveee, if you’re opening this, I’m guessing you feel alone. Maybe it’s one of those days na naiisip mong everyone or everything is against you; maybe people have disappointed you, maybe life feels unfair, or maybe it feels like no matter how hard you try, something always goes wrong. I’d wrap you in the biggest hug possible sana ngayon if magkasama tayo :< I’ll let you know na nandiyan lang ako sa tabi mo, na kakampi mo ako, na hindi mo kailangang maging mag-isa. We both know na life hasn’t always been nice to you my pipilapu. There are days na you might feel everything is going wrong, na everything is not going according to plan. I see your struggles, love; I see and acknowledge how hard everything is for you; that’s why I’m so proud of you for always showing up, even when things are super hard. Kahit na may puking inang naiinggit sayo palagi, kahit na minsan sobrang hirap mag-aral ng Japanese, kahit na may mga taong hindi naniniwala sayo, kahit na may mga taong iniisip na wala ka nang ginawang tama, you’ll always have me, oki? Kakampi mo ako palagi love. Kahit magkaaway tayo or magkabati, lagi mo akong kakampi. I see how much strength and effort you put into keeping going, love; I see the parts of you that you think aren’t enough. And despite everything, mahal na mahal pa din kita, love. When everything is sooo full of judgement, I hope that my love would remind you that you are enough. I won’t promise you that life would suddenly become better tomorrow; I can’t stop bad things from happening, and I can’t protect you from every disappointment na papunta satin, but I can promise that as long as you’ll let me, you won’t have to carry everything by yourself. If the whole world feels like it’s going against you, then I hope you’ll remember na hindi ikaw mag-isa; as long as I’m here, you’ll always have one person standing beside you. As always, I love you, love. Mahal na mahal kita palagi.
With love, Alvin
