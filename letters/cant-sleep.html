You Can't Sleep

Hello love, If you’re opening this right now, then maybe it’s one of those nights again where hindi ikaw makatulog kahit na nag-goodnight na tayo sa isa’t isa. Maybe your room is quiet, it’s dark, lahat ng kasama mo sa house tulog na, and your mind just won’t let you rest. Maybe you’re overthinking, maybe you’re worried about us, maybe you’re upset, or maybe you don’t even know why you can’t sleep. I wish I could be there to accompany you love, If I were beside you ngayon, I wouldn’t tell you to force yourself to sleep. I’d quietly lie down with you, hold your hands, hug you, and let you rest on my chest. Me would gently play with your hair while telling you everything is going to be okay :< Lahat ng worries na nasa isip mo love, lahat ng things na takot ikaw mangyari, they’ll still be there pag tulog at pag gising mo, pero hopefully after you’ve had some rest, they’ll feel a little lighter than they do ngayon. Rest muna ikaw, love. I know it’s easier said than done since maybe your mind sometimes convinces you that you need everything sorted out na before you sleep, or maybe masyado marami kang iniisip. Pero love, you deserve rest too, simply just because you’ve made it through today, and I’m proud of you for it. After you read this letter, close your eyes for a bit and imagine mo na we are together, there’s nothing in our hearts holding us back, wala tayong iniisip na problema, na hamon ng buhay; tayo lang dalawa. It’s quiet except for the soft sound ng aircon or fan, tapos we are cuddling, and every now and then I give you a smol forehead kiss. And if you can’t sleep yet, we’d just stay like that. We wouldn’t even need to talk, love. I’d be perfectly happy just holding you until your mind has slowed down and you finally fall asleep. I know we can’t have that right now, not in our situation sa ngayon. Distance is hard sometimes, and there are nights when all I want is to reach over and hold you, remind you that you’re safe, and I really hate that I can’t do any of that through Discord :< Even though I’m not there ngayon, I hope this letter will remind you that you’re not alone, na mahal kita, and I’m here loving you. Maybe I’m already asleep while you’re reading this, maybe I’m still awake; either way, I’ll always love you. I will never get tired of reminding you that I do; I love you through nights when you feel alone, through nights you feel no confidence in yourself, through nights when you doubt if I really love you, through nights that are hard, through nights that are full of arguments, and through nights that are happy. Sleep well and sweet dreams, lovelove ko. I love you. I’ll be with you pag gising mo, waiting for your text. I love you always.
With love, Alvin
