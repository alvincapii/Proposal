You're Having a Bad Day

Hello lovelove koo, If you’re reading this ngayon, you must feel tired after a long day. There are days na things just don’t go the way we want them to. Days when even the smallest things feel so tiring and exhausting. Mga moments na ginagawa mo naman yung best mo pero things still don’t work out, or when you don’t know why you’re feeling this way. I want you to know na it’s okay love, you don’t have to force yourself to be happy agad-agad. You don’t have to pretend that you’re okay. I’m proud of you for making it through the day. Even if you feel like you didn't accomplish enough, even if things didn't go according to plan, even if all you managed to do was get through the day. You still deserve to rest. You still deserve kindness, especially from yourself. If something happened today that hurt you, I wish I could be there to hug you and let you tell me all about it :< I just wish I were there para samahan kita. If you don't feel like talking about your day, that's okay too, love. We can play together, watch something, talk about random things, or just stay on call while we do our own stuff. The quote I want to share with you today, lovelove ko, is this: “You may encounter many defeats, but you must not be defeated”. I hope this reminds you that just because things didn’t go your way doesn’t mean you’ve failed, okay? You can have difficult days, you can feel tired, and you can definitely take a break. You don’t have to win every battle lovelove ko. Sometimes, making it through the day is more than enough, and I’ll always be proud of you for that. I love you so much love. No matter how your day went, I hope you end it knowing that you’re loved by me, not because of what you’ve done or the things you’ve done, not because you were happy today, but simply because you’re you. Rest well my lovelove, you’ve done enough for today okay? I love you, always and always. Mwamwamwa
